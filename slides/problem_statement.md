## P1 · 问题 / The Problem

**给一个搬不动的零件，把它扶到一个它站不住的姿态，并让它留在那儿**
**Hold a workpiece too heavy to lift in a pose it cannot stand in**

- **给定** 任意零件 · 它当前的稳定放置 · 目标位姿 `T*` · 表面上一小片工作区 `W`
  **Given** an arbitrary part · its current stable placement · a target pose `T*` · a work region `W`
- **求 (a)** 一个或多个可 3D 打印、落地、避开 `W` 的支撑实体
  **Find (a)** one or more printable, ground-standing support solids that stay off `W`
- **求 (b)** 一套把零件送到 `T*`、把支撑装到位的**过程**
  **Find (b)** a **procedure** that brings the part to `T*` and the supports into place
- **使得** 撤手之后，零件在工艺施力下不平移、不转动
  **Such that** once released, the part neither slides nor turns under the process

`T*` 不是稳定放置——它是绕零件自身底面的一条边或一个角倾过去的。**几何和过程是同一道题**：装不进去的支撑不是支撑。
`T*` is not a stable placement — it is tipped about an edge or corner of the part's own
footprint. **The geometry and the procedure are one problem**: a support that cannot be
brought in is not a support.

## P2 · 难点一 / Why it is hard (1)

**需求不是一个力，是一个集合**
**The demand is not one force but a set**

工作区上每一点、绕该点法向 30° 锥内每一个可达方向、从零到 `0.5mg` 的每一个力度，
都是必须承受的工况。
Every point of `W`, every reachable direction within 30° of its inward normal, and
every force magnitude from zero to `0.5mg` must be resisted.

**任一允许载荷无法承受，整套承载认证就不通过；已完成的单件几何仍保留并出图。**
**Any unsupported allowed load prevents full load certification; completed individual geometry is still retained and drawn.**

## P3 · 难点二 / Why it is hard (2)

**你不能把零件抬进夹具**
**You cannot lift the part into a fixture**

零件搬不动，所以它只能**原地倾倒**——与地面的接触不断开、不移位。
Too heavy to lift, so it can only be **tipped in place** — ground contact never broken,
never relocated.

机械手把零件推到并保持在 `T*`；每个接触头分别连接到地面，各件支撑沿各自方向依次滑入，终点贴合。当前版本分别校验各支撑固定底脚的独立平衡、连接几何及顺序安装；部分成功单独保留。
不靠撤手后的重力转动建立接触。
The robot holds the workpiece at `T*`. Each contact head has a separate ground support,
inserted along its own direction in a verified order. The current version checks fixed-foot equilibrium per support, complete connection geometry,
and insertion order separately; partial results are retained.
Release-settling does not establish contact.

需要避免把工件抬入固定夹具的安装过程；可从侧面分别装入的模块并不因“模块化”而被排除。
Avoid installation that requires lifting the workpiece into a fixed fixture; separately inserted modules remain admissible.

## P4 · 什么算成功 / What counts as solved

**找一个压强场，让力、力矩、不翻三件事同时成立——对每一次工艺推力。**
**Find a pressure field that answers force, torque and no-tipping — for every allowed push.**

当前工艺力大小为 `0 ≤ t ≤ K=0.5`，同时遍历工作区、允许方向与整个力度区间。
`t=0` 时也必须承受工件重力。`0.5mg` 是上限，不是唯一工况。
The process magnitude ranges over `0 ≤ t ≤ K=0.5`; vary position, admissible direction,
and magnitude. At zero process force the supports must still balance gravity.

三行是同一个形状读三遍：第三行就是第二行，换到**工件加支撑这一整体**落在地板上的地方，那里每个接触都朝上。
换的是隔离体，不只是位置——所以那一行的域叫 `sys_floor`。
The three lines are one shape read three times: the third IS the second, taken over where
**the workpiece AND its supports, as one body**, meet the floor, where every contact points
up. What changes is the FREE BODY, not just the place — which is why that row's domain is
`sys_floor`.

**支撑多边形不来自方程，来自 `F_supp ≥ 0`**——地板不能拉。
**The footprint condition comes not from the equation but from `F_supp ≥ 0`** — the floor
cannot pull.

Step3 用同一组接触力解工件方程 (1)(2)；Step4 固定脚垫后，把工件与每件支撑的六维平衡联立，内部接触使用同一组反力。整体接地凸包条件仍是必要条件，但不能替代逐件验证。见 [Step4](baseline_algo/step4_floor_contact/README.md)。
Step 3 solves the workpiece equations with shared contact reactions. Step 4 fixes the feet
and checks the workpiece and each independent support using those same internal reactions.
The whole-assembly footprint remains a necessary condition, not a substitute for per-body equilibrium.

**重力在力那一行，不在力矩那一行**——它作用在 `c` 上，对 `c` 不产生力矩。
**Gravity is in the force row and not the torque rows** — acting at `c`, it turns nothing
about `c`.

## P5 · 前提 / Assumptions

**超出这些我不负责**
**Outside these I make no claim**

- 工艺力锥：绕逐点内法向 **30° 半角** · Process cone: 30° half-angle about the local inward normal
- 工艺力大小：**`0 ≤ |F_push| ≤ 0.5mg`**，包括零加工力 · Process magnitude includes the entire closed interval, including zero
- 准静态、刚体 · Quasi-static, rigid bodies
- 支撑力足够：`λ ≥ 0`，无上限 · Support force is sufficient: `λ ≥ 0`, unbounded
- 接触单边 `F_supp ≥ 0`，地板摩擦足够（水平力与偏航都交给它）· One-sided contacts, floor friction sufficient (it carries the horizontal force and yaw)
- **忽略支撑质量**；固定脚垫后联立工件与每件支撑平衡 · **Massless supports**; fixed feet are checked with coupled workpiece and per-support equilibrium
- 支撑可打印、不穿模、连到地面 · Support printable, non-intersecting, ground-reaching
- 每个接触头与自身固定底脚连接成**独立刚体**，沿自身方向滑入、终点贴合；Step2 要求工件对支撑的每个实际面压力向下 · Each contact head and its own fixed feet form **one independent rigid support**, inserted along its own direction; every actual contact face must press downward
- `T*` 由工艺给定，不由我们优化 · `T*` is given by the process, not optimised

## 备份页 · 符号表 / Backup — the symbols

`slides/setup/equations/three_equations.png` 顶部已带两行图例，但投影时字号偏小。需要时用这一页。
The figure carries a two-row legend, but small for a projector. Use this page if pressed.

**符号是成对的：左边是你供给的，右边是问题给你的，形状完全一样。**
**The symbols come in pairs: what you supply on the left, what you are given on the right.**

| | 支撑 · the supports | 工艺 · the process |
|---|---|---|
| 压/推的矢量 · the vector | `F_supp` | `F_push` |
| 力臂 · its arm | `r_supp` | `r_push` |

*(2026-09-01 起 `d_supp`、`d_push` 删除：大小和方向合进一个矢量。方向的约束改为文字声明——
`F_supp` 沿该点皮肤自己的内法向，只压不拉。)*

**`F_supp` 和 `F_push` 不是一类东西。** `F_supp` 在积分里面，**每片皮一个**，是个场；
`F_push` 在等号右边，**一次推力一个**，是给定的。
**They are not the same kind of thing.** `F_supp` is a FIELD — one vector per patch, inside
the integral, along the inward normal there. `F_push` is a PARAMETER — one per push, given.

其余 / the rest:

| | |
|---|---|
| `ẑ` | 向上 · up |
| `mg` | 工件重量 · the workpiece's weight |
| `q` | 推在哪一点（在 `W` 上）· where the push lands |
| `c` | 工件质心，两个力臂都从这儿量 · the centre of mass, which both arms are measured from |

**积分域 / the domains.** 域名跟着**隔离体**走，不跟着零件走。前两行的隔离体是**工件**，域是 `supp_obj`——所有压在工件上的东西；
**工件底下那块地板本身算一个支撑**（它压、不能拉），所以倾倒的**支点**天然在这个域里。
第三行的隔离体是**工件加支撑，当成一个物体**，域是 `sys_floor`——这一整体压在地板上的地方：每根支撑的脚，加上工件自己的支点。
写成 `supp_floor` 会漏掉支点（支点不在任何一根支撑上），是错的。下划线不是排版：mathtext 里连字符会渲染成**减号**，`supp-obj` 会读成减法。
The domain follows the FREE BODY, not the part. Rows 1–2 cut around the WORKPIECE, so the
domain is `supp_obj`, everything pressing on it — and since **the floor under the workpiece
counts as a support**, the **pivot** is in it by right. Row 3 cuts around the workpiece AND
its supports as one body, so its domain is `sys_floor`: every support's foot, plus the
workpiece's own pivot, which is on no support. `supp_floor` would drop the pivot. The
underscore is not typography — in mathtext a hyphen renders as a math MINUS.

**一处故意的不严谨 / one deliberate abuse.** `F_supp` 是**压强**（`dA` 就是来积它的），`F_push` 是**力**。
这对名字在**角色**上平行，在**量纲**上不平行。严格写法是 `P_supp`，但那样会毁掉让这页十秒钟能读的配对。
量纲严格的写法现在**任何图上都没有**了——写着 `P λ` 的那一版已删除，所以这处不严谨只在这里声明。
`F_supp` is a PRESSURE — force per unit area, which is what `dA` integrates — while `F_push`
is a force. The pair is parallel in role, not in units. `P_supp` keeps the units honest and
breaks the pairing. Nothing carries the units-honest `P λ` any more — the page that did is
deleted — so this abuse is declared here and nowhere else.

### 四部分与操作流程

- `setup/`：找到目标姿态 `T*`，当前已完成，本轮不修改选姿态流程。
- `obj_supp/`：工件与支撑的力、力矩平衡，正在做；(1)(2) 使用同一组接触力。
- `sys_floor/`：把工件与全部支撑作为一个整体，判断整体压力中心是否在实际接地凸包内。
- `trajectory/`：机械手把工件推到 `T*` 并保持，支撑逐块插入；每块在终点与设计接触
  **完美贴合**，全部到位后机械手撤力。支撑允许多块独立刚体，不要求共用插入方向。

当前只验算 **满加工力 `t = K = 0.5`（工件体重单位）**，考察工作区 `q` 与允许方向 `d`。
**不计算 `t = 0`**；不再把缺少零加工力工况列成漏洞。`[0,K]` 的两端点检验属于可选扩展，
不是当前任务。支撑反力 `λ ≥ 0` 无上限；支撑质量忽略；地面摩擦足以承担水平力与偏航，
不在此增加摩擦模型。制造间隙、释放后自动落位不属于当前问题。
